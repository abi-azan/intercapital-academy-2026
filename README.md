## 1. Atlantic Grupa (ZSE: ATGR) — equity analysis

Fundamental and relative valuation of Atlantic Grupa, one of the largest consumer goods producers and distributors in
South-East Europe. The work included a visit to the Cedevita factory and a Q&A session with the company's investor
relations team.

**Business and opportunities**
- Revenue mix: own brands 64.1% (Cedevita, Argeta, Barcaffè, Donat, Smoki), distribution of third-party brands 27.1%,
  Farmacia pharmacy chain 8.8%.
- International markets are the fastest-growing segment (+33.4% in Q1 2026), led by Germany and Austria.
- Distribution of Podravka brands in Serbia from July 2026 (about €20m a year) adds revenue on the existing network.
- Coffee is the largest category (26.6% of revenue). A falling arabica price is the main lever for margin recovery,
  and short-term hedging means lower prices reach costs quickly.

**Risks**: coffee and cocoa price volatility, EU packaging regulation (PPWR), loss of key distribution partners,
labour cost inflation and the bargaining power of concentrated retail chains.

**Valuation**

| Method | Setup | Value per share |
|---|---|---|
| Relative valuation | 9 listed consumer peers (Podravka, Orkla, Kraš, Wawel, Kofola, Cloetta, Strauss, Clicks, Raia Drogasil); forward 2026/27 consensus and trailing EV/EBITDA and P/E, blended 60/40 | ~€63 |
| Two-stage FCFE | Cost of equity 8.45% (beta 0.78, revenue-weighted equity risk premium of 6.86%), 9.75% high-growth phase, 3% stable growth | €49.4 |
| FCFF DCF | EBITDA margin recovering from 8.8% to 12%, capex cycle 2026–2028, 8.5% discount rate, 2% long-term growth | Slides 17–18 |

**Conclusion**: target price **€58** against a market price of €51, **+13.7% upside**.

## 2. Investment strategy proposal

Individual task: a portfolio proposal for a private client.

- **Mandate**: €500,000, 15-year horizon (funding a child's university education), hard constraint of no more than a
  15% loss in any calendar year.
- **Building blocks**: global equities (MSCI World, EURO STOXX 50, emerging markets ETFs), Croatian blue chips,
  automation and robotics, Croatian commercial real estate, government bonds and physical gold, with a glide path
  that reduces risk as the goal approaches.
- **Analysis**: 2008 stress test against the 15% limit, 15-year projection in five scenarios (central case about
  4.4% a year, roughly €950,000), comparison with term deposits, tax treatment and an implementation roadmap.

## 3. European Hospitality UCITS ETF

Team project: design of a thematic UCITS ETF on European hospitality (hotels, tour operators, airports and travel
infrastructure) for listing on the Zagreb Stock Exchange.

- 36 positions, benchmark BE500P, €50m target AUM, quarterly rebalancing.
- Portfolio P/E 18.9x against 19.6x for the benchmark, with 13.9% net income growth.
- Dividend yield 2.27% trailing, 2.61% forward.
- Risk: parametric 95% annual VaR of 18.6% (1.21x the benchmark), cross-checked with Monte Carlo and historical VaR.
- Liquidity: at 10% of daily volume, about 23% of the portfolio can be sold the same day and 30% within a week.
- Bottom-up TER and listing cost model (management, custody, HANFA, SKDD and ZSE fees).

The deck is not published because much of it is Bloomberg PORT output.
